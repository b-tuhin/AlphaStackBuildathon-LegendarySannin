1. What PhoneMail is

PhoneMail is an email service that maps your phone number to an email address (yournumber@phonemail.com). You can send and receive email using that address from the web portal, mobile app, or any standard SMTP client.

2. Your account

  • You must provide a valid phone number to create an account. Your phone number becomes your email address and is the primary identifier for your account.
  • You are responsible for keeping your password confidential and for all activity that occurs under your account.
  • You must be at least 13 years old to use PhoneMail.
  • You may not create accounts on behalf of others without their explicit consent.

3. Acceptable use

You agree not to use PhoneMail to:

  • Send spam, bulk unsolicited messages, or content that violates anti-spam laws.
  • Transmit malware, phishing content, or any malicious code.
  • Harass, threaten, or impersonate any person.
  • Violate any applicable local, national, or international law or regulation.
  • Attempt to circumvent the service's security, rate limits, or authentication systems.

4. SMS notifications

When someone sends email to your PhoneMail address and you do not have the mobile app installed (or a push token registered), PhoneMail may send you an SMS notification summarising the message. By creating an account you consent to receiving these transactional SMS messages. Standard carrier messaging rates may apply.

5. Data we collect

  • Phone number — used as your account identity and email address.
  • Password — stored as a bcrypt hash; we cannot recover it in plaintext.
  • Email content — messages you send and receive are stored to power your inbox.
  • Device tokens — if you grant push-notification permission, your device token is stored to deliver notifications without SMS.
  • Login metadata — timestamps and failed-attempt counters used for rate limiting and security; not shared externally.

6. Data we do not collect

  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not serve advertising.

7. Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting support. Upon deletion, email content and personal data are removed within 30 days; anonymised aggregate statistics may be retained indefinitely.

8. Third-party services

PhoneMail may use Twilio to deliver SMS notifications. When you receive an SMS, your phone number is shared with Twilio solely for the purpose of message delivery. Twilio's privacy policy is available at twilio.com/legal/privacy.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, including bcrypt password hashing, short-lived JWT tokens, and rate limiting on authentication endpoints. However, no system is perfectly secure. You are encouraged to use a strong, unique password.
